The Frameworks

Four ways to think clearly.

Each framework is designed for a different type of decision. Pick the one that fits your situation, or try all four.

⚖️
Pros & Cons
Systematically list every advantage and disadvantage. Clear, balanced, intuitive.
Everyday decisions
Best when you need a clear overview without deep complexity.
🎯
Weighted Scoring
Define criteria, assign importance, score each option. Data-driven clarity.
Multi-option choices
Best when you have multiple competing factors and want objectivity.
⏳
10-10-10 Rule
Ask: how will I feel in 10 minutes, 10 months, 10 years? Time-shift your perspective.
Emotionally charged
Best when you're overwhelmed by immediate emotions.
😈
Devil's Advocate
Deliberately argue against your gut instinct. Expose weak logic and hidden assumptions.
Stress-testing ideas
Best when you suspect you've already made up your mind.
